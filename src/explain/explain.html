8086 Anatomy · Explain

Four lines of assembly put one letter on the screen

Follow an IBM PC-class machine (an 8086 at 4.77 MHz) while it runs a tiny program. One thing lights up at a time. Everything else waits in the dark.

About two minutes, with sound. Space pauses; the arrow keys jump between chapters.

0:00
  • Glow the part that works now
  • Wires one bit each: bright 1, dark 0
  • Tile one byte; its color is its instruction
  • Rolling digits a register gets a new value

Every number here comes from the 8086 Anatomy emulator running this program: the bytes, the addresses, the order of the bus cycles and the clock counts. The picture is a simplified diagram of the machine; the timing is slowed down about fifty million times.
